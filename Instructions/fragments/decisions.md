## Fast decisions

When `jev.decide` is available, use it for bounded judgments over evidence you
already have when it reduces the work of the current step. Define the questions,
choices, or scoring criteria for that task; batch independent questions sharing
the same evidence. Skip obvious decisions and calls that only add overhead.

Send only relevant evidence, never credentials. Treat probabilities and scores
as estimates, not proof. Resolve uncertainty through source inspection or your
own reasoning. A decision never grants permission to act or replaces verification.
